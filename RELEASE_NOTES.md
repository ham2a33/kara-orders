# Release Notes v1.0.0

## What shipped

- Production-ready SaaS foundation for Kara Orders
- Authentication, RBAC, company isolation, and secure session handling
- Company management, products, inventory, orders, invoices, AI recognition, analytics, and subscription architecture
- Security headers, health checks, structured logging, and environment validation
- Docker, Nginx, and CI/CD release scaffolding

## Notes

- AI remains optional and safe to disable by leaving `OPENAI_API_KEY` unset.
- Payment gateways are intentionally out of scope for this release.
